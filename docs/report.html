Summary

Four assessment configurations were fitted to two alternative CPUE indices and the same catch history. Estimated final spawning biomass ranges from 0.57 to 0.85 of unfished spawning biomass across these cases. Differences reflect both the CPUE specification and assessment assumptions.

Data and methods

Versioned longline records were checked and extracted for 2000–2024. Two CPUE standardisations supplied indices to separate input-preparation jobs. Each input was fitted under two age-structured assessment configurations. Results synthesis verified and combined the four fitted cases.

Methods and diagnostic tables

The CPUE models use a Poisson log link and effort offset. One accounts for year and vessel; the other accounts for year only. The assessment follows ages 0–10+, fits recruitment scale and catchability, and matches annual catches through fishing mortality. Recruitment, maturity and selectivity assumptions are deliberately simple.
